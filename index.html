    Questions about MedAlign

    How long does the MedAlign assessment take?

    The assessment has eight short steps and takes about 4 minutes. Scheduling a consultation afterwards takes about a minute.

    Do I need to create an account to use MedAlign?

    No. You can take the assessment without an account. MedAlign asks for your location and contact details at the very end, after you have seen your matches.

    How does MedAlign decide which clinics to show me?

    The matching engine looks at dozens of your responses, including goals, symptoms, treatment interests, medical considerations, preferred location, lifestyle and desired therapies. Matches are ranked by fit, never by ad spend.

    What can I compare before I choose a clinic?

    You can compare services, pricing, wait times and telehealth options side by side before deciding whether to book directly or request a callback.

    How do clinics receive patients through MedAlign?

    A clinic receives a scheduled consultation only after a patient who matches its chosen criteria explicitly asks to connect. Clinics pay per qualified consultation, with no long term contracts, and patients are not guaranteed.
